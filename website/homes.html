🏠 Reunite Series

Reunite

Compact, efficient, and designed for families rebuilding stability.

The Reunite Series was designed for father–child reunification, transitional stability, and dignified living. Compact, efficient, and built for WA families who need a stable foundation to rebuild from.

Every Reunite home carries a 30% community impact commitment — the highest of any THWA series. Because families rebuilding deserve the most support.

30%
of profits from every Reunite home go directly to WA reunification and community programs. This is structural, not discretionary.

🌉 Bridge Series

Bridge

A flexible, modern design for families transitioning toward ownership.

The Bridge Series is designed for families in transition — moving from instability toward ownership, from crisis toward contribution. Flexible, modern, and purpose-built for the journey.

The Bridge Series also serves professional practice rooms for counsellors, psychologists, youth workers, and NDIS providers — spaces that support the people who support families.

of profits from every Bridge home go directly to WA community impact initiatives — permanently reinvested, not discretionary.
