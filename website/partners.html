How to Partner

Four Steps to Partnership

Becoming a THWA partner is straightforward. We've designed the process to be as clean as the model itself.

1
Submit an Enquiry
Complete the partner enquiry form below. Tell us about your organisation, your community, and what you're trying to achieve.
2
Initial Conversation
We'll have a conversation to understand your needs, your capacity, and whether our model is the right fit for your organisation.
3
Partner Registration
If we're aligned, we'll register you as a THWA partner. You'll receive your partner_id, which activates CRV and JTC pathway access.
4
Start Delivering
With your partner_id active, you can begin configuring homes, accessing the CRF/JTC model, and delivering pathways to your community.
