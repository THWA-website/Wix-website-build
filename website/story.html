Where It Started

A Simple Belief. A Powerful Movement.

Tiny Homes WA began with a simple belief: housing should strengthen people, not break them.

For too long, families across WA have faced a system where stability is out of reach, ownership is impossible, and housing has been treated as a commodity instead of a foundation for life.

"We decided to build something different — something ethical, scalable, and rooted in the idea that people come before profits."

Tiny Homes WA was born from lived experience, community work, and a deep understanding of what families actually need: clarity, dignity, stability, and a fair chance at a future they can own.

We saw the gaps in the system — crisis accommodation overflowing, families priced out of rentals, regional towns unable to house essential workers, community housing providers stretched thin, traditional builds too slow and too expensive.

So we built a model that solves these problems without adding new ones.

The Gaps We Saw

We Saw the Gaps. We Built the Solution.

Every gap in the system became a design requirement. Every problem became a feature of the model.

🏚️
Crisis accommodation overflowing
We built a supply model that can deploy homes faster than traditional construction — meeting demand where it exists, when it exists.
💸
Families priced out of rentals
We built a pricing model with ethical margins, transparent costs, and no hidden fees — making homes accessible without exploiting housing stress.
🌾
Regional towns without housing
We built a modular model that can be deployed anywhere in WA — metro, regional, or remote — without the delays of traditional construction.
🤝
CHPs stretched thin
We built a partner model that gives CHPs clean documentation, deployment-ready homes, and a pathway model they can operate without THWA involvement.
🐌
Traditional builds too slow
We built a modular supply chain that is significantly faster than traditional construction — families get into homes sooner, communities stabilise faster.
🔑
No pathway to ownership
We built the CRF/JTC mechanism — a financial pathway from stability to ownership that doesn't exploit families or create dependency.
The Cawley Code

The Ethic That Shapes Every Decision

The Cawley Code is the moral backbone of Tiny Homes WA. It is the promise that this movement will never drift into greed, never compromise on dignity, and never forget why it exists.

Principle 1
People Over Profits
Housing exists to strengthen people, not extract from them. We price ethically, operate transparently, and reinvest 20% of all profits into WA community impact initiatives. This is not charity — it is structure.
Principle 2
Dignity First, Always
Every home we build must protect dignity. Every pathway we design must honour the people who walk it. Every decision must strengthen families, not systems.
Principle 3
Clean, Light, Scalable
We do not run villages. We do not manage tenants. We do not deliver programs. We stay clean so the movement can scale, and so the founder can remain present for the people who matter most.
Principle 4
Partners Are the Heart of Delivery
We supply the homes, the pricing, the documentation, and the model. Partners deliver the tenancy, support, compliance, and operations. This keeps the system safe, ethical, and sustainable.
Principle 5
Ownership Is the Goal
Stability is the beginning — not the end. The CRF/JTC mechanism exists to help families move from stability to contribution to ownership. Custodianship is the destination.
Principle 6
Legacy Over Ego
This movement is not built for applause. It is built for impact, for families, for WA, and for the next generation. Every decision must outlive the founder.
Principle 7
Protect the Founder, Protect the Mission
A movement collapses when its founder collapses. The model must remain light, clean, and time-protected so the founder never loses the ability to be present for the people who matter most.
The Code
Immutable. Eternal. Non-Negotiable.
The Cawley Code is not branding. It is not marketing. It is the ethic, the promise, the standard, and the spine of everything Tiny Homes WA stands for — and everything the next custodian will inherit.
