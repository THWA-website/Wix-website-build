Common Questions

Questions You Might Have

Do I need to talk to anyone to use the Design Studio?
No. You can explore layouts, finishes, and indicative pricing without entering your details or booking a call. The Design Studio is yours to explore at your own pace.
What is the Standard or JTC pathway?
CRV stands for Standard Supply. It's our core, charity-ready specification. It requires a registered partner and is designed for community housing delivery.
What is the JTC pathway?
JTC stands for Journey to Custodianship — also known as the Winifred Filamena line. It's our premium ceremonial tier that activates partner tenancy, support services, and pathway administration. It requires a registered partner.
How do I become a registered partner?
Complete the partner enquiry form on our Partner page. We'll have a conversation to understand your organisation and whether our model is the right fit. If aligned, we'll register you and issue a partner_id.
Are the prices on the website final?
All prices shown are indicative only. Final pricing is subject to configuration, delivery location, site conditions, and current material costs. Contact us for a formal quotation.
Do you deliver outside Perth metro?
Yes. We deliver across Western Australia — metro, regional, and remote. Delivery costs vary by location. Use the Design Studio to get an indicative delivery estimate, or contact us for a site-specific quote.
What does "20% of profits to community" mean?
Twenty percent of Tiny Homes WA net profits are permanently allocated to WA community impact initiatives — financial reset, employment readiness, mental health support, family stability, and long-term independence. This is structural, not discretionary.
Can I configure a home without committing to anything?
Absolutely. The Design Studio is a no-pressure exploration tool. You can configure, save, share, and export a quote without any obligation. We'll only contact you if you ask us to.
