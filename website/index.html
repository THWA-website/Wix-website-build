The Cawley Code

The Ethic That Shapes Everything

The Cawley Code is the moral backbone of Tiny Homes WA. It is the promise that this movement will never drift into greed, never compromise on dignity, and never forget why it exists.

01
People Over Profits
Housing exists to strengthen people, not extract from them. We price ethically, operate transparently, and reinvest 20% of all profits into WA community impact initiatives.
02
Dignity First, Always
Every home we build must protect dignity. Every pathway we design must honour the people who walk it. Every decision must strengthen families, not systems.
03
Clean, Light, Scalable
We do not run villages. We do not manage tenants. We do not deliver programs. We stay clean so the movement can scale, and so the founder can remain present for the people who matter most.
04
Partners Are the Heart
We supply the homes, the pricing, the documentation, and the model. Partners deliver the tenancy, support, compliance, and operations. This keeps the system safe, ethical, and sustainable.
05
Ownership Is the Goal
Stability is the beginning — not the end. The JTC mechanism exists to help families move from stability → contribution → ownership. Custodianship is the destination.
06
Legacy Over Ego
This movement is not built for applause. It is built for impact, for families, for WA, and for the next generation. Every decision must outlive the founder.
